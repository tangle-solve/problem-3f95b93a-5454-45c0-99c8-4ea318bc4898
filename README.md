### CHEM-000397: Room-temperature Pr(V) persistence beyond the demonstrated protective ligand environment
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Isolate a room-temperature-persistent molecular Pr(V) complex beyond the demonstrated heavily shielded imidophosphorane environment. Measure decomposition kinetics and establish its electronic-state assignment.
- **Status:** Molecular formal Pr(V) was isolated at low temperature in 2025. A 2026 ligand comparison found modest redox-potential changes but major stability differences, with some Pr(V) states observable only electrochemically.
